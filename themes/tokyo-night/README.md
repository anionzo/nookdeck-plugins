# Tokyo Night

A Nookdeck theme pack: Night, Storm and Moon (dark), and Day (light). Use **Auto** to follow the phone's light or dark setting: Day by day, Night at night.

- A port of the Tokyo Night colours. The palettes are from [folke/tokyonight.nvim](https://github.com/folke/tokyonight.nvim) (Apache-2.0); the original design is by [Enkia](https://github.com/enkia/tokyo-night-vscode-theme) (MIT).
